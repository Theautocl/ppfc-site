How it works

Assessment to follow-up

Site assessment

A daytime visit as part of your first-night callout. We walk the boundaries, find sign, map safe firing lanes and neighbours, and agree a plan with you.

Paperwork done for you

Landholder authority signed and police notified. You don't chase anything.

Night session

Thermal optics, typically 4 hours from dusk. Every shot taken with a clear backstop and positive identification of the target.

Follow-up

We tell you how many were taken and whether a repeat visit is worth it, timed to when it will have the most effect.
